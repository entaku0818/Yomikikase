# Playback stability — 2026-10-26

Scope: iOS narrator, especially VOICEVOX stop/restart and preview switching.

- Cancelled playback exits before starting synthesis or reading the next shared prefetch task.
- A late quota error from cancelled synthesis cannot emit a stale event or clear the new playback's prefetch.
- AVAudioPlayer cancellation is scoped to a playback UUID; a delayed cancellation callback cannot stop its replacement.
- TextInputView rejects cancelled tasks and events from a different playback generation.

Validation: Xcode 27.1, iPhone 18 Pro Max simulator (iOS 27.0), VoicevoxSpeechPlayerTests + VoicevoxTextChunkerTests: 19 passed, 0 failed. New regressions cover immediate stop without synthesis and delayed quota failure after replacement playback. The first test build failed because Xcode script sandboxing could not read Firebase Crashlytics/run; the successful local Debug invocation used ENABLE_USER_SCRIPT_SANDBOXING=NO without changing project settings.

Limits: no production synthesis latency benchmark, real-device audio interruption test, or App Store submission was performed. Kokoro still generates the remaining full text before playback (TextInputView.playWithKokoro and KokoroEngine.synthesize). Time to first audio, cancellation of ongoing local generation, and incremental playback need separate measurement/design; this patch does not claim to accelerate inference.
