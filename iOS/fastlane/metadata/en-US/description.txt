Turn reading time into listening time. Voice Narrator reads PDFs, EPUB ebooks and web articles aloud, so you can catch up on documents during your commute or study while doing chores. Import your content, choose a voice and listen at your own pace.

Standard voices generate speech on your device and can read imported text offline. No Voice Narrator account is required.

7 WAYS TO ADD CONTENT
- Text input: type or paste any text and listen instantly
- PDF: import documents, books, and reports
- Web pages: paste a supported article URL and listen
- EPUB: listen to ebooks from your library
- Google Drive: select supported files through Google Drive in the Files app
- Scanner: point your camera at printed text and listen
- TXT files: import plain text from any source

REAL-TIME TEXT HIGHLIGHTING
Follow the text as standard voices read it aloud, including in PDFs. Highlighting helps you keep your place while studying or checking pronunciation. Highlighting availability depends on the voice you use.

DESIGNED FOR ACCESSIBILITY
Built for users with dyslexia, visual impairments, or reading fatigue. Adjustable speed, pitch, and multiple voice options help everyone find their ideal listening experience.

PLAYBACK CONTROLS
- Reading speed: 0.5x to 2x
- Voice pitch adjustment
- Background playback — keep listening while using other apps

CHARACTER VOICES
Listen in anime-style Japanese character voices such as Zundamon. Character voices are generated on our server, so they need an internet connection. Free plan: 5,000 characters/month. Premium: 200,000 characters/month.

PERSONALIZATION
- Custom dictionary: correct mispronounced names, technical terms, and brand names
- 10+ languages supported
- Save frequently used texts for quick replay

WHO IT'S FOR
- Students reviewing notes or textbooks hands-free
- Professionals catching up on documents during commutes
- Language learners improving listening comprehension
- Anyone with dyslexia, low vision, or reading fatigue
