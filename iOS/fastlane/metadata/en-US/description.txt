Turn PDFs, web articles and ebooks into listening time. Voice Narrator reads your text and DRM-free EPUB books aloud while you commute, do chores or rest your eyes.

ADD YOUR CONTENT
Paste text, import PDF or TXT files, open EPUB books, paste web links, scan printed documents or import supported Google Drive files through the Files app. Some files and web pages may not support text extraction. Google Drive requires its app to be set up and signed in.

LISTEN YOUR WAY
Adjust reading speed and voice pitch. Follow highlighted text with standard voices, use the sleep timer and save content in My Files. Add words to your dictionary to customize pronunciation. Available voices depend on the language and your device. Japanese character voices use VOICEVOX; Kokoro AI voices support Japanese and English.

PREMIUM
Listen without ads, save unlimited files and read texts longer than 4,000 characters to the end. Premium also includes up to 200,000 characters of Japanese character voices per month.

Monthly and annual subscriptions are available. Prices and eligible free trials appear on the purchase screen. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel in Settings > Subscriptions.

Standard voices are generated on your device. Character voices send text to our server to generate audio. Web articles and Google Drive require an internet connection.
