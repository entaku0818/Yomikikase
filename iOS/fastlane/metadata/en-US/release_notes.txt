New features
- Download Japanese literary classics from Aozora Bunko and listen to them right away. Tap "Classics" on the Home screen to pick from 40 public-domain works by Soseki Natsume, Ryunosuke Akutagawa, Osamu Dazai, Kenji Miyazawa and more
- Ruby readings and editorial notes are stripped automatically, so the text is read aloud the way it is meant to sound
- Added a sleep timer. Stop after 5-60 minutes or at the end of the chapter, with a gentle fade-out
